Settings

Providers — works out of the box, no account needed

frenz ships with free keyless providers already set up: install it, open a conversation, she answers. Providers are tried top to bottom — if one is rate-limited, capped, or down, the next takes over mid-send. Free providers may log or train on your conversations, and their limits change without warning.

Optional upgrade: keyless models are smaller, so personas are simpler and the subtext lands less reliably. Adding a free key (Gemini, Groq, Cerebras, OpenRouter — no card required) makes them sharper; an Anthropic key below is the full experience.

Anthropic (Claude) — optional, the quality ceiling

Notifications

When someone texts you first, their message lands on the main screen with an unread badge. Turn this on and you'll also get a nudge when the app is closed.

Time

The app runs on real time — her clock, night looseness, openers, and due-date follow-ups all track it. Skip ahead when you don't want to wait for tonight. Time only moves forward.

Your data

All friends, conversations, and each friend's private impressions of you are saved automatically on this device.

frenz is a private, local-first app. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except your messages to the Claude API using your own key.
