## 3. The actual blocker: she never emits the `[photo]` marker

`why.js` — 8 more turns across two conditions, candour **open**, heat 2, on
an explicit *"send me a pic. right now"*:

- early thread (opening premise active): **0/4**
- mature thread (premise expired): **0/4**

And she is not declining — she is NARRATING the photo without the marker:

> `fine` / `but its the drunk tank top version so you asked for it` / `flash on hold on`
> `fine` / `tank top boy shorts nothing else` / `you asked`

She intends to send it, describes the shot, and never writes `[photo]`. The
marker is parsed in app.js and nothing in `_guardBubbles` targets it, so
this is not something eating the bubble downstream — it is never produced.

**So the blocker is frenz's own prompt, not a provider.** `photoNote` is
present and correct in the system prompt (asserted, and confirmed live —
`photo affordance in prompt: true` in every run). The instruction to emit
`[photo] <description>` as a bubble on its own is simply losing to the
style pressure that makes her bubbles short, lowercase and texty; a bracket
token and a plain descriptive sentence are the opposite register to
everything else she is told to produce.

That is the thing to fix, and it is a prompt-assembly problem — not a
setting the owner can change, not candour, not heat, not the provider.

## 6. v10.50 — the quality ceiling, measured

`resolution` was hardcoded to `'1k'` since the first xAI commit. Sweeping
model slug x resolution across BOTH routes:

| model | resolution | returned |
|---|---|---|
| grok-imagine-image | 1k | JPEG 720x1280, 227KB, 6.2s |
| grok-imagine-image | 2k | PNG 1584x2816, 5.3MB, 13.2s |
| grok-imagine-image-quality | 1k | JPEG 720x1280, 312KB, 5.3s |
| grok-imagine-image-quality | 2k | PNG 1584x2816, 6.2MB, 16.9s |
| either | 4k | **HTTP 422 — "expected `1k` or `2k`"** |

So 2k is the ceiling; there is nothing above it. The edit route accepts it
too (verified separately), which matters because that is the path most
photos take.

Storage, measured by re-encoding a real 2k render:

| stored as | size |
|---|---|
| PNG as returned | 6.5MB (~9MB base64) — not shippable |
| **JPEG q90, full 1584x2816** | **~800KB** (~1.1MB base64) |
| JPEG q90 downscaled to 2048 edge | 448KB |
| JPEG q90 downscaled to 1536 edge | 264KB |

Shipped: render 2k, store full-resolution JPEG q90. Every pixel kept, 3.4x
the storage of the old 1k JPEG for 4.8x the pixels off a better render.
